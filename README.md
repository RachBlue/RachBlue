### What I do

I'm a graphic designer who vibe codes websites and apps, with AI as part of how I build. I run marketing for a four-location building-supply company in New York and Connecticut and take freelance design and web projects on the side. Most days that means designing something, building the page it lives on, and making sure the tracking behind it actually works. I use Claude to build faster, and I review and fix everything it writes before it ships.

- **Web:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, WordPress and Elementor
- **E-commerce:** Shopify themes and Liquid
- **Analytics and tracking:** GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, conversion tracking, UTMs
- **Design:** Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop and InDesign, Canva, CapCut
- **Building apps:** Supabase, Vercel, Expo, and Claude as a pair programmer

I studied graphic design, then started building the things my designs lived on: flyers and logos for friends, Wix sites for people I knew, then the website for the construction company where I spent four years as a project coordinator. These days it's Shopify storefronts, landing pages and apps. Coordinating job sites taught me to ship on deadline and keep a lot of people in the loop, and I still work that way.
